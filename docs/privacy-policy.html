Yona — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Yona is a subscription tracker that helps you keep all your subscriptions, their costs, and renewal dates in one place. This policy explains what information Yona collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you create an account you provide an email address, which is used to sign you in and secure your data.
  • Content you create. The subscriptions you add — names, costs, billing cycles, renewal dates, notes — and any documents or images you attach to them. This content is stored so it syncs to your account across launches and devices.
  • Purchase information. If you buy Yona Premium, your purchase is processed by Apple. We receive confirmation of your entitlement status (whether you have Premium) but never your payment card or billing details.

Yona does not use any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app: store and sync your subscriptions and documents to your account.
  • To authenticate you and keep your data secure.
  • To display brand logos for the services you track.
  • To unlock and restore your Premium purchase.

Third-Party Services

Yona relies on a small number of service providers to operate. Data is shared with them only as needed to provide the app:

  • Supabase — hosts our backend: account authentication, your subscription data, and uploaded documents. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Brandfetch — when Yona displays a logo for a service you track, the service or brand name is sent to Brandfetch to look up its logo. See Brandfetch's privacy policy.
  • Apple & RevenueCat — process and manage in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives purchase and receipt information to determine your entitlement status. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app or by contacting us at the address below; deleting your account removes your subscriptions and uploaded documents from our systems.

Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our backend provider under their security controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children's Privacy

Yona is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
