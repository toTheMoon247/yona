Frequently asked questions

How many subscriptions can I track?

Yona is free for up to 4 subscriptions. Yona Premium unlocks unlimited subscriptions with a single one-time purchase — no recurring fee.

Is Yona Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time purchase. You pay once and it's yours.

I bought Premium on another device — how do I get it here?

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases. Your Premium unlock is tied to your account and Apple ID.

How is my data stored?

Your subscriptions and any documents you add are stored securely with your account and synced across your devices. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account or data?

You can delete your account from within the app, or email us at the.yona.app@gmail.com and we'll remove your data.
